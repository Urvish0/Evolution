### Intelligence

- The complete operational state of an AI system at a given point in time.
- Intelligence is composed of multiple versioned artifacts including prompts, memory, retrieval configuration, tool usage, model configuration, policies, evaluations, and metadata.

### Artifact

- A versioned component that contributes to Intelligence.
- Artifacts evolve independently while remaining associated with an Intelligence Commit.

### Replay

- The process of reconstructing a previous Intelligence Commit to reproduce historical behavior.
